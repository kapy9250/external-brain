# White Papers on Surveillance and Tracking
*Source: Sara Konecny (@signal2prompt)*
*URL: https://x.com/signal2prompt/status/2030280873275224376*

Anyone else reminded of this scene (posted)? Anyway, for those who are curious, here's some white papers that Varien references: 

- **wifi signals detecting movement:** "See Through Walls with WiFi!" [web.stanford.edu/class/cs244/pa](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs244/papers/wifiseethrough.pdf) 
- **keystroke cadence:** "Keystroke dynamics as a biometric for authentication" [sciencedirect.com/science/articl](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S016740480300007X) 
- **gait identification:** "Gait Identification Using Accelerometer on Mobile Phone" [thanghoang.github.io/publication/12](https://thanghoang.github.io/publication/12-m_gait/) 
- **ultrasonic beacons & retailers:** "Privacy Threats through Ultrasonic Side Channels on Mobile Devices" [ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/79619](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/7961965) 
- **license plate readers:** "Vehicle trajectory reconstruction from automatic license plate reader data" [journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/15](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/1536867X17735398) 
- **holding your phone:** "New Behavioral Biometric Features for Continuous Authentication of Smartphone Users" [arxiv.org/abs/1501.01199](https://arxiv.org/abs/1501.01199) 

🌟 Bonus: 
- **no wifi? no problem, you can still be tracked:** "Robust Magnetic Fingerprint Positioning in Complex Indoor Environments Using Res-T-LSTM" [mdpi.com/1424-8220/25/2](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/25/2/411)
